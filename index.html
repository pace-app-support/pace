PACE — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7th of October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PACE handles your information when you use the PACE mobile application ("the App").

We are based in Australia and handle personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

Contact: support.pace.app@gmail.com

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Account identity. When you sign in with Apple, Apple shares a unique user identifier and, on first sign-in only, your name and email address. You may hide your email using Apple's private relay.
  • Profile information. Display name, username, bio, training goal, experience level, weekly training distance, and any social links you add (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Strava).
  • AI Coach messages. The questions you type to the AI Coach (see Section 4).

Information collected automatically

  • Training data. Workouts generated and completed, training week, streak, distance completed, preferred training days.
  • Workout feedback. The difficulty rating you give a session and any free-text note.
  • Recovery data. Whether Injury Mode or Sick Mode is active.
  • Learning progress. Which in-app lessons you have completed.
  • Nutrition entries, if you use the Fuel feature.

What we do NOT collect

  • We never see or store your payment card details. All purchases are handled by Apple.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers.

2. Public Community Information

PACE includes a Communities feature. If you set a username, the following is stored in Apple's CloudKit public database and is visible to other PACE users:

  • Display name and username
  • Bio
  • Training goal
  • Total workouts completed, total distance, current streak
  • Any social links you add

Please do not include sensitive personal information in your bio. If you never set a username, no profile is published and you will not appear in Communities.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Generate and adapt your training plan
  • Track your progress and streak
  • Provide the Communities feature
  • Provide the AI Coach
  • Send training reminder notifications (only if you enable them)
  • Respond to support requests

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. The AI Coach and Third-Party AI

The AI Coach is an optional feature. When you send a message to it, the App sends a summary of your training information to Google's Gemini API so a response can be generated. That summary includes:

  • Your training goal, experience, weekly distance and recent PB
  • This week's planned workouts and whether they are completed
  • Your recent workout difficulty ratings and notes
  • Whether Injury Mode or Sick Mode is active
  • Your learning progress
  • Your nutrition totals, if you have logged any

You supply your own Google Gemini API key, which is stored securely on your device in the iOS Keychain and is never sent to us. Google's use of this data is governed by Google's own privacy policy and terms.

If you do not want your training data sent to Google, do not use the AI Coach. The rest of the App works without it.

5. Who We Share Information With

  • Apple — for Sign in with Apple, iCloud/CloudKit storage, and App Store purchases and subscriptions.
  • Google — for the AI Coach, as described in Section 4.

We may also disclose information where required by law.

6. Storage, Security and Retention

Where data is stored. Your training data is stored locally on your device. Your community profile is stored in Apple's CloudKit public database, which may be hosted on servers outside Australia.

How we protect it. Data in transit is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS. CloudKit storage is protected by Apple's infrastructure. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

How long we keep it. We keep your information for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, your community profile is removed and your local data is erased.

7. Your Rights and Choices

  • Access and correct your profile information in the App at any time.
  • Change your notification preferences in the App or in iOS Settings.
  • Delete your account and all data at any time via More → Preferences → Account → Delete Account. This permanently removes your community profile and erases your local training data. Deleting your PACE account does not remove the record Apple holds of your Sign in with Apple use; you can revoke PACE's access under Settings → Apple ID → Sign in with Apple on your device.

Australian users may request access to, or correction of, their personal information, or make a complaint, by emailing support.pace.app@gmail.com. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

Users in the EU/UK have additional rights under the GDPR/UK GDPR, including the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to processing, and the right to data portability. Our lawful bases for processing are performance of a contract (providing the App) and your consent (the AI Coach). You may also lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

8. Children

PACE is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
