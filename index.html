Getting Started

Setting up your plan

When you first open PACE, you'll choose your training goal, your experience level, roughly how much you run each week, and how many days you can train. PACE builds your first week from those answers.

You can change any of this later in More → Preferences.

Your training week

Each week contains a mix of sessions — easy runs, intervals, tempo work, long runs and rest days. Every session has a title, a target effort, and a breakdown of the warm-up, main set and cool-down.

Tap Mark Complete after a session. You'll be asked how hard it felt — this helps PACE understand how you're responding to training.

Training phases

PACE structures your plan across phases — base, build, deload, specific, peak and taper. Your plan adjusts automatically as your training week advances.

AI Coach

Setting up the AI Coach

The AI Coach is optional and powered by your own free Google Gemini API key.

  1. Create a free key at aistudio.google.com
  2. In PACE, go to More → Preferences → AI COACH
  3. Paste your key and tap SAVE KEY

Your key is stored securely on your device in the iOS Keychain and is never sent to us. The Coach can then see your training plan, your recent sessions, your difficulty ratings and notes, and whether you're in recovery mode.

You can remove your key at any time from the same screen.

The Coach isn't responding

  • Confirm your key is saved in More → Preferences → AI COACH
  • Check your internet connection
  • If your key was revoked or has run out of quota, create a new one at aistudio.google.com and save it again

Training & Recovery

Injury Mode and Sick Mode

In More → Preferences → Recovery you can turn on Injury Mode or Sick Mode. PACE holds back your training and sends lighter recovery reminders until you switch it off. Only one can be active at a time.

Changing your goal or training days

Go to More → Preferences → Training Plan. Choose your new goal and how many days you can train, then confirm. Your plan rebuilds and your training week resets to 1.

Changing your preferred training days

Go to More → Preferences → Preferred Training Days and pick the days you want to train. PACE will schedule your sessions on those days.

Communities

Joining the community

  1. Go to More → My Profile → Edit
  2. Choose a username and add a short bio
  3. Tap SAVE PROFILE
  4. Open Communities — your profile is now published

Adding your social links

In the same edit screen, add your YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook or Strava. You can paste a full link, or just your handle. They'll appear as tappable icons on your profile.

Removing your profile from Communities

Clear your username in More → My Profile → Edit and save. Your profile is removed from the public list.

Subscription

What PACE Pro includes

PACE Pro unlocks the full training system, including your personalised training plan, the AI Coach and progress insights.

Managing or cancelling your subscription

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple.

  1. Open the Settings app on your device
  2. Tap your Apple Account at the top
  3. Tap Subscriptions
  4. Select PACE and choose to cancel or change plan

Cancelling stops the next renewal. You keep access until the end of the period you've already paid for.

Restoring a purchase

If you've reinstalled PACE or signed in on a new device, open the subscription screen and tap Restore Purchases at the bottom.

I was charged but don't have access

Tap Restore Purchases first. If that doesn't work, email us with the date of purchase and we'll sort it out.

Requesting a refund

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple Account.

Account & Data

Deleting your account

Go to More → Preferences → Account → Delete Account. This permanently removes your community profile and erases all your local training data. It cannot be undone.

Deleting your PACE account doesn't remove the record Apple holds of your Sign in with Apple use. You can revoke PACE's access under Settings → Apple ID → Sign in with Apple on your device.

Signing in

PACE uses Sign in with Apple or Google. There are no passwords to remember. Tap the relevant button on the first screen — the first sign-in creates your account.

Reporting a bug or requesting a feature

Email us at support.pace.app@gmail.com with a short description. Including your device model and iOS version helps us track it down faster.

Reporting inappropriate content

If you see a community profile that breaks our guidelines, email us at support.pace.app@gmail.com with the username and a description. We review every report.

Notifications

Turning training reminders on or off

In PACE: More → Notifications. Or via your device's Settings → Notifications → PACE.

I'm not receiving reminders

  • Check notifications are allowed in your device's Settings
  • Check nothing like Focus or Do Not Disturb is blocking them
  • Make sure you've completed onboarding — reminders begin after that
